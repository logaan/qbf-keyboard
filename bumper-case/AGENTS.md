# Bumper case work

- Keep the lower blue plate, orange cover and upward blue guard sources, native FreeCAD solids, STEP/STL exports, previews and reports inside this folder. Keyboard geometry is a dimension and collision reference; do not embed it in the case CAD.
- Commit source and generated outputs as work progresses. Do not add code comments.
- Edit `parameters.json` and `build.py`, then run `./bumper-case/build.sh` from the repository root. It validates single solids, closed print meshes, z=0 print placement, fasteners and modeled operating clearances before saving the case.
- Run `./bumper-case/render.sh` after case geometry changes. It checks the original KiCad solids and model bounds, creates temporary assembly boards, and generates native KiCad images. It must not modify the original PCB files or component assignments.
- The two recurring assembly previews are `generated/assembled.png` (with keycaps) and `generated/inspection.png` (without keycaps). **Only the ten keycap models may disappear. Keep all ten Choc switches, the display, its socket, controller, battery, connectors, reset/power controls, boards, fasteners and feet and all three printed case parts in both.** `prepare_views.py` checks the visibility difference and writes `generated/preview-components.json`.
- Keep the reset casing enclosed, with a close roof and a circular access hole over the recessed button. Keep the power casing enclosed, with a slot clearing the full 2 mm slide stroke and the actuator standing above the roof. Verify native STEP geometry as well as the operating envelopes; the reset model has raised corner posts.
- Preserve full thumb-key travel and side access while fitting control covers. Keep the lower blue and orange case bottoms at the main PCB TOP z=0. No case material may extend below z=0 or overhang the enlarged PCB outline. Main PCB and baseplate still touch directly. Print both lower parts upright from their PCB-contact faces, and the upper guard upright from its mating face; never restore the old 180-degree print flips. The original 301055 pack sits in a MAIN-PCB-ONLY well on the intact baseplate with 0.2 mm insulating adhesive. Never cut the battery opening in the baseplate. Keep a continuous 1 mm battery roof, not just an inter-row canopy.
- Run `python3 bumper-case/render_parts.py` in a Python environment containing NumPy and Pillow to refresh the case-only, underside and print-orientation images. These images come directly from the exported STL geometry.
- Inspect both assembly previews and case-only views after generating them. Commit the images, CAD exports, reports and scripts together. Update `README.md` if dimensions, print supports, assembly order or regeneration commands change.
- `reference/placements.json` records PCB placements and source hashes. If upstream electronics change, use KiCad's Python to rerun `capture_reference.py`, review the dimensions, rebuild and verify again. Do not bypass a changed reference hash.
- Retain the outer M2 pair at (100.5, 8.75)/(100.5, 46.25) in the fixed assembly frame, equivalent to (102,10.25)/(102,47.75) from the enlarged PCB corner mm, fully inside the orange part. The upper guard is clamped at the four left mounts using 0.4 mm supported flanges and 1.2 mm DIN439 M2 thin nuts. Check nominal 15 mm Choc shoulders at z=2.2 to 3.0 mm; the upper shoulder reliefs are 15.3 mm through z=3.1 mm. Note the 0.025 mm nominal nut/shoulder side gap for physical fit testing.
- Battery geometry is recorded in `../ergogen-kikad/design/battery.json`, PCB footprint metadata, `parameters.json` and the captured assembly reference. Keep them aligned. After PCB changes run the native STEP floor proof (`./scripts/check-battery-well.sh`) and mechanical regeneration before final case checks and images.

- Upper guard upright walls belong only on the perimeter border above the PCB, with curved descending ends near the landscape thumb keys. Do not restore inter-column dividers or a wall between portrait and landscape keys. Keep low plate webs and mounting flanges below cap travel. A full roof over the current JST housing cannot clear conservative full key travel; leave it accessible unless the component stack changes and is revalidated. Verify right fasteners against native reset/power housings AND the orange upright walls. Require at least 1 mm wall clearance; the present nut-to-wall gap is 2 mm.

- Keep the fixed case layout frame separate from physical PCB coordinates. The PCB is 117.25 × 58 mm, with bounds [-1.5,-1.5,115.75,56.5] in case coordinates. Capture origins and bounds from generated board geometry. Keep the 114.25 × 55 mm reference width/height as layout spans, not PCB size. Never silently shift component envelopes when the board border changes.
- Use 0.3 mm battery roof clearance: underside z=2.4, top z=3.4. This puts the bridge underside 0.2 mm above the guard mating plane for 0.2 mm layer printing. Preserve the shallow battery recess, and document bridge/support limitations rather than claiming support-free printing. Internal fillets must clear native components and full key travel.
