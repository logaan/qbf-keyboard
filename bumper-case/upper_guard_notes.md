# Upper guard integration

`upper_guard.py` contains only case geometry. It does not embed component or PCB solids. The parent builder supplies the lower-left case solid as a profile reference and adds the returned upper guard to the case document and exports.

Call `build_upper_guard(parameters, reference, lower_left_shape)`. `guard_dimensions(parameters, reference)` returns the roof and flange dimensions for the verification report. `guard_mount_specs(parameters, reference)` identifies the four unchanged left mounts and their new nut elevations.

The required `upper_battery_envelope` parameter is `[x, y, z, width, depth, thickness]` in millimetres above the main PCB datum. It must describe the entire protected, wrapped battery. Do not supply the bare-cell thickness or silently reuse the original 3.5 mm battery with a smaller CAD envelope. Optional dimensions are `upper_battery_roof_clearance` (0.2 mm), `upper_battery_roof_thickness` (1.0 mm), and `upper_guard_top` (10.9691 mm).

The continuous roof covers the entire original protected 220 mAh 301055 battery, bridges into the battery-corridor borders, and stops before the JST connector. The frozen battery envelope is `[1.65, 22.25, -1.4, 57.5, 10.5, 3.5]`. A 58.1 × 11.1 mm opening at `[1.35, 21.95]` passes through the main PCB only. The unchanged baseplate forms the floor at z=-1.6 mm; 0.2 mm adhesive places the battery bottom at -1.4 mm and its top at 2.1 mm. The roof underside is at 2.3 mm and its top at 3.3 mm, leaving 0.2 mm battery clearance and 0.7 mm beneath the conservative pressed-cap underside at z=4.0 mm. Insulate the FR4 opening edges and avoid pressure or sharp edges against the pouch. Battery, PCB opening and assembly-model coordinates must remain aligned.

Outer 1 mm walls and 1 mm rails in the two 2.55 mm inter-column gaps rise to the nominal resting cap top. The rails clear cap sidewalls by 0.775 mm. The socket/controller junction is open where an upright wall would interfere with the display assembly. Cap corner relief maintains at least 0.15 mm clearance to the conservative full-travel boxes.

The real switch shoulder reserves 15 × 15 mm between z=2.2 and z=3.0 mm, independently of the simplified assembly model. Eight 15.3 mm shoulder reliefs extend from the guard mating face through z=3.1 mm; the 14.1 mm body apertures continue above. The guard has at least 0.1 mm clearance to those nominal shoulders. The simplified KiCad switch model places its flange at a different height and cannot substitute for this check.

The entire lowest guard face is at z=2.2 mm, touching the blue lower part's plate top. Print this face down, without the 180-degree rotation used for the original two case parts. The recessed battery roof is a short bridge above that face; inspect bridging or provide removable supports beneath it. Keep supports and surface defects away from the battery. The four compression flanges are 0.4 mm thick, intended as two 0.2 mm layers, and are supported directly by the lower case after assembly. Use a nozzle capable of the specified 1 mm walls.

The lower-left H1-H4 bosses must end at z=2.2 mm without the previous z=1.8 mm nut recess. Four DIN 439 M2 thin hex nuts, 4 mm across flats and 1.2 mm thick, now sit on the guard at z=2.6 mm and end at z=3.8 mm. Standard 1.6 mm thick nuts do not fit this arrangement. [DIN 439 dimensions](https://www.aspenfasteners.com/content/pdf/Metric_DIN_439_spec.pdf) support the thin-nut choice. After the shoulder reliefs trim the compression flange, it retains approximately 9.106 mm² of nut bearing area. The pocket radius is 2.5 mm; fit the nuts before the caps and use an appropriate thin tool. Orient the hex nut with its 4 mm across-flats width along x. The nominal gap between the adjacent 15 mm switch shoulders is 4.05 mm, leaving only 0.025 mm on either side of a 4 mm nut. This is a nominal fit with almost no lateral tolerance; inspect real switches and nuts in the first physical assembly before tightening. Printed shoulder relief alone cannot establish the clearance between purchased switch and nut hardware.

The nominal M2 screw length is 7.0 mm from beneath its head: 3.2 mm of touching PCBs plus 2.6 mm to the nut seat plus 1.2 mm nut thickness. A 7 mm screw ends flush with the nominal nut top. An 8 mm screw would extend into the conservative pressed-cap envelope and needs shortening; check actual hardware, printer tolerance and thread engagement before use. The relocated right mounts retain their separately specified hardware.

Development verification used the frozen original-battery envelope above. The result was one valid solid and a closed mesh, zero overlap with either case half, ten switch-body envelopes, eight left switch-shoulder envelopes, ten full-travel cap envelopes, the battery envelope, the JST and display socket. Minimum cap clearance was 0.15 mm, battery clearance 0.2 mm, shoulder clearance 0.1 mm and socket clearance 0.28 mm, with zero nut overlap and 9.106 mm² bearing area at all four left mounts. Repeat the complete assembly checks after regenerating the boards and case exports. The fixed 0.025 mm nominal switch-shoulder/nut gap remains a physical-fit limitation.
