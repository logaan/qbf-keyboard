# Upper guard integration

`upper_guard.py` contains only case geometry. It does not embed component or PCB solids. The parent builder supplies the lower-left case solid as a profile reference and adds the returned upper guard to the case document and exports.

Call `build_upper_guard(parameters, reference, lower_left_shape)`. `guard_dimensions(parameters, reference)` returns the roof and flange dimensions for the verification report. `guard_mount_specs(parameters, reference)` identifies the four unchanged left mounts and their new nut elevations.

The required `upper_battery_envelope` parameter is `[x, y, z, width, depth, thickness]` in millimetres above the main PCB datum. It must describe the entire protected, wrapped battery. Do not supply the bare-cell thickness or silently reuse the original 3.5 mm battery with a smaller CAD envelope. The current settings are `upper_battery_roof_clearance` = 0.3 mm, `upper_battery_roof_thickness` = 1.0 mm, and `upper_guard_top` = 10.9691 mm. The clearance should be explicit in `parameters.json`; the module's fallback remains 0.2 mm.

The continuous roof covers the entire original protected 220 mAh 301055 battery, bridges into the battery-corridor borders, and stops before the JST connector. The frozen battery envelope is `[1.65, 22.25, -1.4, 57.5, 10.5, 3.5]`. A 58.1 × 11.1 mm opening at `[1.35, 21.95]` passes through the main PCB only. The unchanged baseplate forms the floor at z=-1.6 mm; 0.2 mm adhesive places the battery bottom at -1.4 mm and its top at 2.1 mm. At the specified 0.3 mm battery clearance, the roof underside is at 2.4 mm and its top at 3.4 mm, leaving 0.6 mm beneath the conservative pressed-cap underside at z=4.0 mm. Insulate the FR4 opening edges and avoid pressure or sharp edges against the pouch. Battery, PCB opening and assembly-model coordinates must remain aligned.

Only the original key-layout perimeter carries upright walls. There are no rails between any columns and no upright wall along the case split between the portrait and landscape keys. The low plate and four compression flanges connect the part structurally. The left and front/back 1 mm perimeter walls reach the nominal resting cap top; the front/back walls descend toward the thumb-key area along a cubic Bezier curve from x=63.3 mm, z=10.9691 mm to x=75.3 mm, z=3.4 mm, with horizontal tangents at both ends. The control points in the x/z plane are `[63.3,10.9691]`, `[67.5,10.9691]`, `[71.1,3.4]`, `[75.3,3.4]`. All guard geometry above z=3.8 mm remains outside the original rounded 114.25 × 55 mm layout outline; intersecting it with that outline extruded upward produces zero overlap volume. This exclusion uses the original key-layout outline, not the enlarged physical PCB outline.

The enlarged PCB bounds are `[-1.5,-1.5,115.75,56.5]` in the unchanged layout datum. The upper guard stays in place: its walls at x=-1.2..-0.2 mm and y=-1.2..-0.2 / 55.2..56.2 mm are now supported above that PCB border through the lower case. Keep the reference `width` and `height` as the 114.25 × 55 mm layout dimensions and store enlarged physical bounds separately. The upper guard still begins at its z=2.2 mm mating plane; the lower blue and orange parts now start at the PCB top z=0 and no case skirt extends below it.

The battery roof has 0.3 mm plan corner radii and 0.2 mm upper-edge fillets. It ends smoothly at x=59.55 mm before the battery plug and JST connector. A complete JST roof cannot preserve the conservative full key travel: its body spans x=66.15..73.75 mm, y=24.55..30.45 mm, z=0..4.85 mm while pressed cap undersides reserve z=4.0 mm above y=0.5..27 and y=28..54.5. A roof above the connector would intrude into those cap envelopes; the remaining inter-row strip is only 1 mm wide. Leave the connector open instead of adding a token strip or reducing key travel.

The real switch shoulder reserves 15 × 15 mm between z=2.2 and z=3.0 mm, independently of the simplified assembly model. Eight 15.3 mm shoulder reliefs extend from the guard mating face through z=3.1 mm; the 14.1 mm body apertures continue above. The guard has at least 0.1 mm clearance to those nominal shoulders. The simplified KiCad switch model places its flange at a different height and cannot substitute for this check.

The entire lowest guard face is at z=2.2 mm, touching the blue lower part's plate top. Print this face down; all three case parts now print upright. The recessed battery roof remains an 11.7 mm short bridge across the plate's open battery corridor. Its underside is 0.2 mm above the guard's print plane: with 0.2 mm initial layers, the roof begins as a bridge on the second layer. The roof top is coplanar with the surrounding low plate at z=3.4 mm. Inspect the slicer preview and test bridging on the chosen printer and material; do not let automatic underside filling consume battery clearance. Conventional removable support is difficult within the shallow recess, so do not add support blindly; use a removable interface only if the slicer can preserve the designed underside, and remove every trace before assembly. Keep support residue, bridge droop, roughness and pressure away from the battery. An additional underside fillet would not eliminate the bridge and risks consuming battery clearance, so the underside remains unchanged. The four compression flanges are 0.4 mm thick and supported directly by the lower case after assembly. Use a nozzle capable of the specified 1 mm walls.

The lower-left H1-H4 bosses must end at z=2.2 mm without the previous z=1.8 mm nut recess. Four DIN 439 M2 thin hex nuts, 4 mm across flats and 1.2 mm thick, now sit on the guard at z=2.6 mm and end at z=3.8 mm. Standard 1.6 mm thick nuts do not fit this arrangement. [DIN 439 dimensions](https://www.aspenfasteners.com/content/pdf/Metric_DIN_439_spec.pdf) support the thin-nut choice. After the shoulder reliefs trim the compression flange, it retains approximately 9.106 mm² of nut bearing area. The pocket radius is 2.5 mm; fit the nuts before the caps and use an appropriate thin tool. Orient the hex nut with its 4 mm across-flats width along x. The nominal gap between the adjacent 15 mm switch shoulders is 4.05 mm, leaving only 0.025 mm on either side of a 4 mm nut. This is a nominal fit with almost no lateral tolerance; inspect real switches and nuts in the first physical assembly before tightening. Printed shoulder relief alone cannot establish the clearance between purchased switch and nut hardware.

The nominal M2 screw length is 7.0 mm from beneath its head: 3.2 mm of touching PCBs plus 2.6 mm to the nut seat plus 1.2 mm nut thickness. A 7 mm screw ends flush with the nominal nut top. An 8 mm screw would extend into the conservative pressed-cap envelope and needs shortening; check actual hardware, printer tolerance and thread engagement before use. The relocated right mounts retain their separately specified hardware.

Development verification used the frozen original-battery envelope above with the earlier 0.2 mm roof-clearance setting. The result was one valid solid and a closed mesh, zero overlap with either case half, ten switch-body envelopes, eight left switch-shoulder envelopes, ten full-travel cap envelopes, the battery envelope, the JST and display socket. After removing all internal uprights, minimum guard-to-cap clearance was 0.2858 mm, battery clearance 0.2 mm, shoulder clearance 0.1 mm and socket clearance 0.28 mm, with zero nut overlap and 9.106 mm² bearing area at all four left mounts. Regenerate and repeat complete assembly checks for the larger PCB, z=0 lower-case bases, and explicit 0.3 mm roof clearance; the earlier development check does not verify those combined changes. The fixed 0.025 mm nominal switch-shoulder/nut gap remains a physical-fit limitation.
