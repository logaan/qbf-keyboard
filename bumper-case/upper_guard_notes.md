# Upper guard integration

`upper_guard.py` contains only case geometry. It does not embed component or PCB solids. The parent builder supplies the lower-left case solid as a profile reference and adds the returned upper guard to the case document and exports.

Call `build_upper_guard(parameters, reference, lower_left_shape)`. `guard_dimensions(parameters, reference)` returns the roof and flange dimensions for the verification report. `guard_mount_specs(parameters, reference)` identifies the four unchanged left mounts and their new nut elevations.

The required `upper_battery_envelope` parameter is `[x, y, z, width, depth, thickness]` in millimetres above the main PCB datum. It must describe the entire protected, wrapped battery. Do not supply the bare-cell thickness or silently reuse the original 3.5 mm battery with a smaller CAD envelope. Optional dimensions are `upper_battery_roof_clearance` (0.2 mm), `upper_battery_roof_thickness` (1.0 mm), and `upper_guard_top` (10.9691 mm).

The continuous roof covers the entire battery footprint, bridges into the existing battery-corridor borders, and stops before the JST connector. With 0.2 mm adhesive, a 2.2 mm pack and 0.2 mm overhead clearance, its underside is at 2.6 mm and its top at 3.6 mm. Full key travel ends at a 4.0 mm cap underside, leaving 0.4 mm. At those assumptions, a protected pack thicker than 2.4 mm cannot retain the required 1 mm roof and 0.2 mm cap travel clearance. The builder rejects that arrangement. Battery selection and its assembly model must be updated together before release.

Outer 1 mm walls and 1 mm rails in the two 2.55 mm inter-column gaps rise to the nominal resting cap top. The rails clear cap sidewalls by 0.775 mm. The socket/controller junction is open where an upright wall would interfere with the display assembly. Cap corner relief maintains at least 0.15 mm clearance to the conservative full-travel boxes.

The entire lowest guard face is at z=2.2 mm, touching the blue lower part's plate top. Print this face down, without the 180-degree rotation used for the original two case parts. The recessed battery roof is a short bridge above that face; inspect bridging or provide removable supports beneath it. Keep supports and surface defects away from the battery. The four compression flanges are 0.4 mm thick, intended as two 0.2 mm layers, and are supported directly by the lower case after assembly. Use a nozzle capable of the specified 1 mm walls.

The lower-left H1-H4 bosses must end at z=2.2 mm without the previous z=1.8 mm nut recess. Four DIN 439 M2 thin hex nuts, 4 mm across flats and 1.2 mm thick, now sit on the guard at z=2.6 mm and end at z=3.8 mm. Standard 1.6 mm thick nuts do not fit this arrangement. [DIN 439 dimensions](https://www.aspenfasteners.com/content/pdf/Metric_DIN_439_spec.pdf) support the thin-nut choice. The compression flange has approximately 9.70 mm² of nut bearing area. The pocket radius is 2.5 mm; fit the nuts before the caps and use an appropriate thin tool.

The nominal M2 screw length is 7.0 mm from beneath its head: 3.2 mm of touching PCBs plus 2.6 mm to the nut seat plus 1.2 mm nut thickness. A 7 mm screw ends flush with the nominal nut top. An 8 mm screw would extend into the conservative pressed-cap envelope and needs shortening; check actual hardware, printer tolerance and thread engagement before use. The relocated right mounts retain their separately specified hardware.

Development verification used a provisional protected battery envelope `[0.9, 22.25, 0.2, 57.5, 10.5, 2.2]`, not a claim that a battery of that size exists. The result was one valid solid and a closed mesh, zero overlap with either case half, ten switch-body envelopes, ten full-travel cap envelopes, the proposed battery envelope, the JST and display socket. Minimum cap clearance was 0.15 mm, battery clearance 0.2 mm, socket clearance 0.28 mm, with zero nut overlap and 9.70 mm² bearing area at all four left mounts. Repeat those checks with the selected battery and newly generated boards; do not treat this provisional test as final assembly verification.
