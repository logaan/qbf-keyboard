# Recessed XIAO board assembly

The routed main PCB and circuit-free baseplate are 97.7 × 58.5 mm, nominally 1.6 mm each, with four M2 holes. The main board has a protected 301055 battery well and an open-right XIAO notch; the baseplate remains intact beneath the battery and most of the controller. Its separate L-shaped opening gives access to the insulated BAT+ lead.

Fabrication must support **0.2 mm copper-to-routed-edge** on the controller hand-solder lands. Battery-well copper clearance remains **0.5 mm**. The supplied XIAO has native plated castellations; the host uses ordinary top copper lands, not fabricated castellations. The controller notch has at least 0.2 mm nominal clearance even for a conservative square module outline. Measure the actual module, PCB thickness and routing tolerance before fabrication.

Support the XIAO coplanar on 0.05 mm insulating film over the baseplate, with film cut away for the underside BAT+ wire. Its PCB top is z+0.05 mm relative to the main PCB top. Hand-solder its fourteen standard castellations to the paired host lands using controlled bridges; keep the nine interleaved unused Plus pads clear. The proof reserves bridges 0.8 mm wide and at most 0.8 mm high, with 0.42 mm minimum clearance to other native castellations. Prove this soldering process on a sample before a full build.

Connect JST-SH pad 1 to pack positive and pad 2 to pack ground; confirm factory cable polarity. PCM12 common pad 2 feeds VBAT at BW1; throw pad 3 receives pack positive and pad 1 is unused. An insulated ≤0.6 mm OD wire runs from BW1 through the baseplate access to the XIAO underside BAT+ pad. Module ground uses castellation 13. USB powers and charges through the XIAO's native circuit; the battery switch must be on to charge the connected pack. Leave the native approximately 50 mA charge setting unchanged for this small protected pack.

Use four M2 × 5 mm low-head screws (heads at most Ø3.8 × 1.6 mm), with 4 mm AF × 1.6 mm nuts underneath. The proof gives 2.4 mm minimum full-travel cap clearance above these heads. The connector reserve clears the cap travel by 1.0 mm; the battery clears by 1.9 mm. The integrated antenna has a 3 mm host copper/component keepout on both boards, verified against native filled copper. Its closest switch-housing clearance beyond that region is recorded in the contract.

`./scripts/generate.sh` runs native DRC and the native-STEP battery-floor check transactionally. `./scripts/check-assembly.sh` checks actual PCB holes/notch solids, native copper, all 23 module castellations, full cap travel, fasteners, battery, connector, power stroke, USB cable allowance and onboard-reset probe access. `generated/assembly-contract.json` records the fixed coordinate frame, board and source hashes, component poses and operating keepouts for the later case stage. Supplier dimensions, simplified models and physical trial limits are explicit there and in `models/README.md`; the nominal envelope proof is not physical prototype validation.
