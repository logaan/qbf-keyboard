# KiCad assembly models

`mbk_15u.wrl` converts Max Burger's [original MBK 1.5U rendering mesh](https://github.com/mburger89/mbk-3dmodels/tree/30af24fdc6267b08dfaa644add2eff9ad6aa5c55), centered without scaling and placed at an assumed 7 mm resting underside height. The author's rendering-only, noncommercial terms are retained in `source/MBK_README.md`. The mesh is not manufacturing or mating geometry; it does not validate cap travel or the internal cavity.

KiCad's installed STEP models supply the JST-PH header, C&K power switch, and 6 mm reset button. Their offsets match the PCB footprints. Generated Choc, nice!nano, nice!view, battery, socket and mating-plug models are dimension references with simplified details, not supplier CAD. The nano reserves 36 × 18.3 mm and a 6.8 mm assembled top; the display reserves 36 × 14 × 2.9 mm with its lowest underside at 8 mm. The original battery uses the maximum 57.5 × 10.5 × 3.5 mm envelope, centered at (30.4, 27.5), resting on 0.2 mm insulating adhesive above the intact baseplate. Its bottom is at z=−1.4 and top at z=2.1 inside the main-PCB-only opening. Dimensions and the short lead reference are recorded in `../battery.json`; the pouch, wrap and protection details remain approximations. The display socket uses an assumed 6.5 mm housing plus 2.5 mm header stack. Purchased socket heights need checking against these targets.

Choc reference dimensions follow the [Kailh mechanical drawing](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/5114/5114_CPG135001D03.pdf); display bounds and contacts follow the [nice!view dimension drawing](https://nicekeyboards.com/static/2f2592ff1ff8021932814b20cb76e3c4/821da/niceview_dimensions.png). The nominal 15 mm Choc shoulder now occupies z=2.2 to 3.0 mm above the PCB; the earlier rendering-only shoulder height has been corrected. Colors are illustrative. The 3D preview shows nominal resting placement, not confirmed full-travel clearance. In particular, the inter-row connector beneath cap overhangs still requires a physical travel check.

Rebuild models with `./scripts/build-3d-models.sh`, regenerate boards with `./scripts/generate.sh`, then render with `./scripts/render-3d.sh`. KiCad renders both PNGs. `inspection-3d.png` hides caps and the display; the PCB, switches, battery, controller and sockets remain at their assembled positions. Open `generated/pcbs/macropad.kicad_pcb` in KiCad's 3D Viewer to orbit the assembled model.
