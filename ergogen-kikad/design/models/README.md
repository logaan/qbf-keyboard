# KiCad assembly models

Active assignments live in `scripts/attach_models.py`. `./scripts/build-3d-models.sh` rebuilds the reference models and exports the actual circuit-free baseplate. `./scripts/render-3d.sh` produces native KiCad assembled and inspection PNGs; inspection hides only the ten caps.

The installed KiCad STEP models supply the C&K PCM12SMTR and JST-SH SM02B-SRSS-TB header. Native envelopes include the power locating pegs, actuator and connector metal tails. The SH mating plug is a conservative allowance, not mating supplier CAD; the complete reserve is x65.05..74.15, y45..51, z0..3 mm. Confirm the purchased plug and wire bend fit this reserve.

`xiao_plus_reference.wrl` uses the official Seeed PCB outlines and component footprint bounds from `../sources/XIAO-Plus.kicad_pcb`, with simplified height references. It is not manufacturer assembly CAD. The shared Plus/Sense project includes microphone and IMU footprints; those conservative optional envelopes are retained without asserting their population on the non-Sense Plus. Small passives remain within the module plan. Nominal module thickness is the project's 1.6 mm; measure the supplied module before manufacture. The recessed module rests on 0.05 mm insulating film above the baseplate; matching film and baseplate openings expose the underside BAT+ wire landing. Solder bridges are controlled 0.8 mm wide by at most 0.8 mm high references.

`mbk_15u.wrl` converts Max Burger's [MBK 1.5U rendering mesh](https://github.com/mburger89/mbk-3dmodels/tree/30af24fdc6267b08dfaa644add2eff9ad6aa5c55), centered without scaling. The author's rendering-only, noncommercial terms remain in `source/MBK_README.md`. `mbk_1u_reference.wrl` is an independent 17.5 × 16.5 mm envelope reference with a simplified cavity, not a scaled 1.5U mesh. Both rest at assumed underside z7 and top z10.969114; the clearance proof sweeps the conservative full cap boxes down 3 mm to underside z4. Actual cap cavity, stem mating and bottom-out require a physical trial.

Choc references follow the [Kailh drawing](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/5114/5114_CPG135001D03.pdf): 15 mm shoulders at z2.2..3, simplified body, pegs and pins. Clip compliance is not modelled. Battery, insulated leads and M2 hardware are dimensional references. Buy M2 screws with at most Ø3.8 × 1.6 mm heads and 5 mm under-head length, with 4 mm AF × 1.6 mm nuts. The protected battery maximum envelope is 57.5 × 10.5 × 3.5 mm at (35.5,48), bottom z−1.4, top z2.1, on 0.2 mm insulating adhesive over the intact battery floor.

The baseplate reference comes from native board-only STEP, normalized to the nominal 1.6 mm full stack because that export omits outer copper/mask thickness. Historical nice!nano, display and PH models remain unassigned legacy assets. `bounds.json` lists only currently generated references. Sources and nominal operating envelopes are in `../assembly.json`; the reproducible native-solid/copper proof is `./scripts/check-assembly.sh`, with its result and case-stage contract under `generated/`.
